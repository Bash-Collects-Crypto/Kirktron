Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. holidays). Orders placed after 2:00 PM CT may begin processing the following business day. You will receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a second email with tracking information once it ships.

During new set releases, major card shows, and the November–December holiday period, processing can take up to 5 business days. We will always post a notice when that happens.

Estimated Delivery Times

We stock our protective supplies across multiple fulfillment partners, so delivery time depends on which warehouse holds your item. The warehouse is chosen automatically to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Shipping MethodProcessingTransit Time
Standard (U.S. warehouse items)1–3 business days3–7 business days
Standard (overseas warehouse items)1–3 business days8–16 business days
Expedited1–2 business days5–10 business days

Total time to your door = processing time + transit time. Transit estimates are business days and do not include weekends or holidays.

Larger items such as wall-mounted display cabinets and framed multi-slab cases are shipped separately from small accessories and may arrive on a different day than the rest of your order. You are never charged extra for a split shipment.

Shipping Costs

Shipping options and costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and the contents of your cart. You will see the exact cost before you pay — we never add shipping charges after an order is placed.

Where We Ship

We ship to all 50 U.S. states, including Alaska, Hawaii, and APO/FPO addresses. Delivery to Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories may add 3–7 business days.

We do not currently ship internationally. If you are outside the United States and want to order, email us and we will tell you honestly whether we can get it to you.

Tracking Your Order

A tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order ships. Tracking can take 24–72 hours to show its first scan — this is normal and does not mean your package is lost. If tracking has not updated in 7 business days, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

Delays Outside Our Control

Once a package is handed to the carrier, delivery is in their hands. Weather, customs inspections, carrier backlogs, and incorrect addresses can all cause delays. We will help you chase down any late package, but we cannot guarantee carrier delivery dates.

Wrong or Incomplete Addresses

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will reship it once you cover the reshipping cost. If you catch the mistake before the order ships, email us immediately and we will correct it at no charge.

Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages

If tracking shows delivered but you do not have the package, check with neighbors and your local carrier first, then contact us within 7 days. If your item arrives damaged, send us photos of the item and the outer packaging within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it free of charge.
