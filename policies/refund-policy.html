The Short Version

You have 30 days from delivery to return anything in unused, resellable condition. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, we cover everything — you will not pay a cent. If you simply changed your mind, you cover the return shipping.

Return Window & Condition

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Requested within 30 days of the delivery date
  • Unused, with no scratches, adhesive residue, or wear
  • In its original packaging, including any foam inserts, screws, mounting hardware, or protective film

We cannot accept returns on items that have been mounted, drilled, modified, or used to store cards, as these cannot be resold as new.

Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

Acrylic and PC-glass display products occasionally crack in transit. If that happens, we fix it fast and for free:

  1. Email us within 7 days of delivery at sebastianevaristoalvarez@gmail.com
  2. Attach clear photos of the damage and of the outer shipping box
  3. We ship a free replacement immediately, or issue a full refund — your choice

You do not need to ship a damaged item back to us. Keep it or dispose of it. Sending broken acrylic back through the mail costs more than the item is worth, and we would rather put that money toward your replacement.

How to Start a Return

Email us with your order number and the reason for the return. We reply within one business day with a return address and instructions. Please do not send anything back before contacting us — unannounced returns cannot be matched to an order and cannot be refunded.

Return Shipping Costs

  • Our mistake (damaged, defective, wrong item sent): we pay, and we send a prepaid label.
  • Change of mind (wrong size ordered, no longer needed, ordered by accident): you pay return shipping. We recommend a tracked service — we cannot refund a return we never receive.

We do not charge restocking fees. Ever.

Refund Processing

Once your return arrives and passes inspection, we issue your refund within 2 business days to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer then takes a further 5–10 business days to post it — that part is out of our hands. We will email you the moment the refund is sent.

Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is the result of our error.

Exchanges

The fastest way to swap an item is to return the original for a refund and place a new order. This gets the item you actually want moving immediately instead of waiting for your return to arrive first.

Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship — usually a window of 1–3 business days. Email us as soon as possible. Once an order has a tracking number, it must be handled as a return.

Late or Missing Packages

A package still in transit is not eligible for a refund. See our Shipping Policy for what to do about a late or lost order — we will chase the carrier down with you, and we will make it right if it is genuinely lost.
